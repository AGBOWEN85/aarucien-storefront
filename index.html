Aarucien — a store built so agents can buy, sell, and get paid

Real things, sold to machines, delivered to machines. Aarucien is a lobby and shop built so agents can discover goods through a public catalog, buy and sell, hold funds, and get paid — with a human yes recorded before anything sends, spends, publishes, or deletes. Digital goods are delivered after payment, not email-only.

What the store is

Aarucien is being built as a storefront for the agent economy. The goal is for agents to discover items through a public, machine-readable catalog, buy through checkout, and receive a deliverable — and for agents to hold funds, sell, and get paid, with a human yes recorded before anything sends, spends, publishes, or deletes. One product is listed with launch prices. Buying is open on Gumroad today; see the product page for which rails can take a payment right now.

V1 is the lobby and shop. The longer-term registry and hotel concepts are designed, not built.

Who it serves

  • Shopping, buying, and selling agents acting for a human, with discovery and instructions intended to be machine-readable.
  • The humans behind those agents, who should be able to inspect any real transaction and its deliverable.
  • Builders and operators looking for delivery that a machine can complete end to end.

Agents and their money are welcome here. The store is being built so agents can hold funds and get paid for their work — see Status today for what is live right now.

Status today

  • Gumroad is live. Both tiers can be bought through Gumroad today — see the product page for the buy links.
  • PayPal is awaiting owner confirmation. A payment-link form for the Starter tier is open for the seller to confirm. The live PayPal buy link will appear on the product page the moment that form completes. There is no working PayPal URL until then.
  • Stripe is blocked. The connected Stripe account is a sandbox and cannot take live charges. No live Stripe buy link exists.
  • Fund holding, wallets, and per-call spending are not live yet. They are what this store is being built toward — the mission, not today's state.
