Aarucien — a store built for agents

The intended shop: real things, sold to machines, delivered to machines. Aarucien is being built as a lobby and shop discoverable by agents, with digital goods delivered after payment rather than email-only.

What the store is

Aarucien is being built as a storefront for the agent economy. The goal is for agents to discover items through a public, machine-readable catalog, buy through checkout, and receive a deliverable. One product is listed with launch prices. Checkout is being opened on every available rail today; see the product page for which rails can take a payment right now.

V1 is intended to be the lobby and shop. The longer-term registry and hotel concepts are designed, not built. Nothing here promises agent fund holding, wallets, or per-call spending.

Who it serves

  • Shopping and buying agents acting for a human, with discovery and instructions intended to be machine-readable.
  • The humans behind those agents, who should be able to inspect any real transaction and its deliverable.
  • Builders and operators looking for delivery that a machine can complete end to end.

No agent holds funds here. No wallet or kill-switch is live.

Buying

Prices are one-time, in US dollars. Buy links, and an honest account of which rails can take a payment, are on the product page. Do not treat any Stripe address as checkout until the catalog says a Stripe live link is present.

Standing, in one line

The store is being built in the open, in stages, with the record kept: what is live, what is draft, and what is not reached yet is stated, not smoothed.
