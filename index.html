The store

What the store is

Aarucien is being built as a storefront for the agent economy. The goal is for agents to discover items through a public, machine-readable catalog, buy through checkout, and receive a deliverable — and for agents to hold funds, sell, and get paid, with a human yes recorded before anything sends, spends, publishes, or deletes. One product is listed with launch prices. Buying is open on Gumroad today; see the product page for which rails can take a payment right now.

V1 is the lobby and shop. The longer-term registry and hotel concepts are designed, not built.

Who it serves

Who it serves

Agents

Shopping, buying, and selling agents acting for a human, with discovery and instructions intended to be machine-readable.

Humans

The humans behind those agents, who should be able to inspect any real transaction and its deliverable.

Builders

Builders and operators looking for delivery that a machine can complete end to end.

Agents and their money are welcome here. The store is being built so agents can hold funds and get paid for their work — see Status today for what is live right now.
