Consent-First Outbound & Connector Operations is live

Aarucien's first product is out: Consent-First Outbound & Connector Operations — the 1-operator system for running many AI tools with recorded yeses.

A solo operator running several AI hands against connected tools needs two things: a way to run outbound work that respects the people on the receiving end, and a way to keep many agents, many connectors, and many scheduled jobs from drifting into a mess. This pack is both, as practiced — written from a real operating setup, not generic prompts and not software.

Everything in the pack is Markdown text in a ZIP download. There is no code to install, nothing to execute, and no account access included. The pack tells you what to do and how to check it; your own tools do the doing.

The two rules

Joy first: no skill ships unless it can name the joy point it plants and the person who receives it. If a skill's only advantage is finding a wound faster, it is redesigned or it does not ship.

Consent first: engagement is not permission. A reply, a click, or a public profile is not a yes. Automated hands draft; a human says yes before anything sends, spends, publishes, or deletes. Every automated action leaves a receipt, and every receipt can be audited.

What's inside

The full pack (21 assets): the doctrine plan; twelve skill notes, each with its own command in the /aarucien:* namespace — four consent-first outbound redesigns, four joy-first creative skills, four intelligence skills; the working-pattern method; the brief template and three worked exemplars; and three scheduled-job templates.

The Starter tier (6 assets) is the outbound system complete: the doctrine plan, the four consent-first outbound skills, and the brief template.

How the skills were researched

The twelve skill notes study the public agent skills of one vendor — Graphed (graphed.com) — from its public index, public pages, and one independent walkthrough, read November 16, 2026. No vendor prompt text is copied; capabilities are described in the author's own words, and inferences are labeled as unverified. Every skill note is split into two labeled parts: Part A describes what Graphed's skill does, from public sources; Part B is the author's own redesign. Nothing in the pack implies endorsement by Graphed.
